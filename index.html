Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly will my server be activated?

After your payment is confirmed, we provide your server within 24 hours. If we fail to deliver within 24 hours, we will refund double the amount you paid.

What if you do not deliver my server within 24 hours?

If we fail to provide your server within 24 hours after confirmed payment and valid payment proof, we will refund double the amount you paid to the original payment method where possible.

Can I choose Ubuntu, Debian, or Nginx?

Yes. On every VPS plan use the Operating system dropdown (Ubuntu 24.04, Debian 12, Rocky, Ubuntu + Nginx, Ubuntu + Docker, or Custom). Your choice is sent with your payment proof.

Can I choose Paper, Forge, or Fabric for Minecraft?

Yes. On every Minecraft plan pick server software (Paper, Purpur, Spigot, Vanilla, Fabric, Forge, NeoForge). We install what you selected after payment verification.

Can I install Minecraft plugins and mods?

Yes — Paper/Purpur/Spigot support plugins; Forge/Fabric/NeoForge support mods on compatible plans.

Do VPS plans provide root access?

Yes. Linux VPS plans include full root / SSH access.

Do you provide backups?

Automated backups are included on Minecraft Pro and Premium. Ask support about VPS snapshots.

Is the server created automatically when I click Buy?

Not fully automatic yet. You choose plan + OS/software, pay, and email proof — we then deploy the exact OS/software you selected and send your IP + login.
